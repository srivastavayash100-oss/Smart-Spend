SMART SPEND

Personal Expense Tracker

Manual Expense Logging (No Auto-Fetch)

This is a premium manual spend tracker. It does not scan your SMS or fetch backend transactions automatically. You own your data by logging each outlay yourself, keeping you actively conscious of every transaction.

Behavior-First Financial Gamification

Your money habits are linked to a tier system from Bronze to Legend. Instead of punishing restrictions, the algorithms scoring process awards structural trophies for long-term consistency, game-ifying data parameters.

Sandbox Local Database (100% Offline)

Unlike commercial trackers, your financial statements never touch a remote cloud server. The ledger architecture is compiled strictly inside your browser's local sandbox storage, guaranteeing total security.

Categorized Budget Parameters

Every transaction is mapped directly into four target parameters: Essentials, Lifestyle, Investments, or Savings. This categorizing system calculates your real-time budget usage balance seamlessly.

Automated Monthly Reset Cycle

On the 1st of every month, the core shell automatically resets itself. It auto-finalizes your previous month's trophies and prompts you fresh to lock a new target. You don't have to manage month transitions manually.

Smart Timeline Continuation (No Backlog Traps)

If you miss tracking for a few months (e.g., you logged in Jan, skipped Feb-Mar, and return in April), commercial apps force you to fill old Jan backlogs first. Smart Spend automatically marks skipped periods as neutral 0-entries and lets you continue instantly from the current active month.

Flexible Manual Finalization

While the system auto-locks your ledger on the 1st, you have complete control to finalize your billing cycle and secure your performance trophies early at any moment directly from the Trophies panel.

Instant Offline PWA Accessibility

Engineered as a Progressive Web App, this engine operates 100% independent of network configurations. It guarantees lightning-fast startups and reliable offline database transactions anywhere.

How Your Trophies Work

Each month you get a hidden score (0-100). That score turns into trophies and slowly moves your league.

Monthly score → Trophies
  • Score 75-100 · +18 trophies
  • Score 55-74 · +10 trophies
  • Score 35-54 · +3 trophies
  • Score 25-34 · 0 trophies
  • Score 0-24 · −8 trophies
Leagues & thresholds
  • 0-29 · Bronze
  • 30-59 · Silver
  • 60-99 · Gold
  • 100-149 · Crystal
  • 150-299 · Master
  • 300-499 · Champion
  • 500+ · Legend
What moves your score
  • Goal usage: Inside or just over your goal helps.
  • Future‑you: More into Investments + Savings = more points.
  • Lifestyle: Very heavy Lifestyle can quietly pull score down.
  • Streaks: 2+ good months in a row boost gains; 2+ heavy months soften losses.
